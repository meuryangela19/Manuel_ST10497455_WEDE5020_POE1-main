About Leumy

About Our Business

Leumy is a retail business that sells products online and in-store, established in March 2024. It specializes in the sale of beauty and fashion products, offering a wide range of products such as cosmetics, skincare products, personal care items and fashionable clothing for women.

Our Mission

To provide high quality personal care, beauty and fashion products at reasonable prices while delivering the best customer experience, by promoting sustainable business growth and customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To be one of the most reliable and customer-oriented beauty and fashion retailer businesses by making the shopping experience better.

What We Offer

Leumy offers a range of beauty and fashion products to meet different customer needs. Our product categories include skincare, cosmetics, personal care and women's fashion clothing.

Our Commitment

We aim to provide customers with affordable products, helpful service and a convenient shopping experience both online and in-store.
